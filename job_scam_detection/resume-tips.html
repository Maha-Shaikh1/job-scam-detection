Resume Tips & Safety Guide

Apply smarter AI-powered scams are evolving — learn how to write a scam-proof resume, spot deepfake recruiters, and protect your data.

⚡

Watch Out — Job Scams Got Smarter !

Fraudsters today don't just post fake jobs — they use AI to write convincing descriptions, run deepfake video interviews, and build replica company websites that look 100% real. A LinkedIn badge or a company logo means nothing anymore. Before you send your resume or sign anything, verify the company on mca.gov.in and call their official HR number directly.

Smart Resume Tips

💡 What You Need To Know

📧

Use a Professional Email

Use firstname.lastname@gmail.com. Avoid nicknames. scammers also target unprofessional-looking profiles on job portals to pitch fake "exclusive opportunities."

Pro Tip
🔒

Never Put Aadhaar on Resume

Never include Aadhaar, PAN, DOB, or home address.data brokers scrape job portals and sell this info. Identity theft cases from resume data have tripled since 2023.

Scam Risk
🤖

Beware of AI Deepfake Interviews

Scammers conduct fake video interviews using AI-generated faces. If a recruiter's video looks slightly "off" or robotic — end the call and verify through official HR email.

Alert
🔗

Add LinkedIn & GitHub

A verified LinkedIn profile adds credibility. real companies run background checks via LinkedIn. GitHub shows actual work — critical for tech roles.

Adds Credibility
📍

City Only, Not Full Address

Write "Mumbai, Maharashtra" — never your full home address. Sharing complete addresses is a serious privacy risk and serves zero legitimate recruitment purpose.

Privacy
🎯

Tailor Resume for Each Role

Match keywords from job descriptions for ATS systems. if a job accepts your generic resume too fast — that's a red flag. Real ATS filters take time.

ATS Friendly
📸

Be Careful With Photos

Avoid photos on online applications. scammers use AI face-swap tools to create fake profiles using your photo. Only include if required by a verified company.

Scam Risk
🧾

Watermark Your Resume PDF

Add a watermark like "Sent to [Company] on [Date]" before sending to unknown companies. Discourages misuse and helps you track where your resume went.

Smart Move
🌐

Verify Company on MCA + LinkedIn

Before applying, check the company on mca.gov.in and LinkedIn. scammers clone entire company websites with fake CIN numbers — always cross-verify both sources.

Updated
Best Practices

✅ Do's & 🚫 Don'ts

✅ Do This

✅Use a clean, single-column format that ATS systems can read easily
✅Quantify achievements — "Increased sales by 30%" beats "Improved sales"
✅Proofread twice — spelling errors signal carelessness to real HRs
✅Save as PDF to preserve formatting across all devices
✅Research the company on MCA21 before sending your resume
✅Follow up via official company email after applying
✅Use JobShield AI to scan offer letters before accepting

🚫 Don't Do This

🚫Don't include religion, caste, or marital status
🚫Don't use tables or graphics — ATS bots can't read them
🚫Don't lie about skills — companies verify everything
🚫Don't send resume to unknown WhatsApp or Telegram numbers
🚫Don't join video interviews without verifying the recruiter's identity
🚫Don't pay any "registration", "kit", or "training" fee — ever
🚫Don't ignore gut feeling — if something feels off, it probably is
