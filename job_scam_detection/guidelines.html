🛡 Job Safety Guidelines

Learn how to stay safe from job scams

Stay One Step Ahead of Scammers

Job scams are increasing rapidly. Awareness is your strongest protection. Follow these guidelines before applying for any job online.

⚠️ Key Safety Tips
🚨

Never Pay Fees

Legitimate companies never charge joining fees, registration, or training costs upfront.

📧

Check Official Email

Real recruiters use company domains. Avoid hiring over Gmail, Yahoo or WhatsApp only.

💰

Unrealistic Salary

Promises of very high income for minimal effort are the most common scam signal.

🔎

Research Company

Verify company on MCA21, LinkedIn, Glassdoor and check registration status before applying.

🪪

Protect Identity

Never share Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, or OTPs during recruitment processes.

⏳

Avoid Urgency

"Limited seats" and "apply in 24 hours" pressure tactics are classic scam red flags.

🌐

Verify Website

Check the official company website, domain age, and HTTPS security before sharing anything.

📄

Read Job Details Carefully

Fake jobs often contain spelling mistakes, vague descriptions, and unrealistic requirements.

🚨 Where to Report Job Scams
🖥️

Cybercrime Portal

India's official government portal to report online fraud including job scams, financial fraud, and identity theft.

Report at cybercrime.gov.in →
📞

National Helpline 1930

Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline immediately if you've lost money or shared personal details with a scammer.

👮

Local Police / FIR

File an FIR at your nearest police station or cyber cell, especially if financial loss has occurred.

🏦

Report to Your Bank

If you've transferred money, immediately call your bank's fraud helpline to freeze or reverse the transaction.

⚡ Act Fast! If you've been scammed, report within 24 hours on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. The faster you report, the higher the chance of recovering lost funds.
