Got Questions?

Frequently
Asked

Everything you need to know about how JobShield works and what it can do for you.

How does JobShield detect job scams?
▾
JobShield uses machine learning and natural language processing to analyze job descriptions, recruiter messages, and other recruitment content. The system looks for suspicious keywords, unrealistic salary promises, payment requests, and patterns commonly used in fraudulent job postings.
Can I analyze WhatsApp messages or screenshots?
▾
Yes. JobShield supports screenshot and document scanning. Using an OCR engine, the system extracts text from images or PDF files and then performs the same AI-based analysis used for normal text input.
What signs usually indicate a fake job posting?
▾
Common warning signs include requests for registration fees, extremely high salaries for simple work, urgent hiring pressure, incomplete company details, and communication through unofficial channels such as random WhatsApp numbers.
Does the system provide a confidence score?
▾
Yes. After analyzing the job content, JobShield provides a prediction result along with a confidence percentage. This helps users understand how strongly the system believes the job posting is legitimate or suspicious.
Who can benefit from using JobShield AI?
▾
JobShield AI is designed for students, fresh graduates, and job seekers who want to verify job opportunities before applying. It helps users avoid recruitment scams and make safer decisions while searching for jobs online.
